02Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate FaithFlow. The following categories of personal data are processed:

Category Examples Purpose

03Religious Data — GDPR Article 9

Special Category Data The data described in Section 2 reveals your religious beliefs and practices. Under Article 9 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), this constitutes special category data and is subject to enhanced protections.

Because FaithFlow tracks prayer frequency, Bible reading, spiritual challenges, and personal reflections, substantially all personal data processed by this application qualifies as special category data under Article 9(1) GDPR.

We process this data solely on the basis of your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR), provided at the time of account creation via two explicit checkboxes:

  • Consent to this Privacy Policy and the processing of your religious practice data
  • Confirmation that you are 18 years of age or older

You may withdraw this consent at any time by deleting your account (see Section 8). Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

Data location: All personal data is stored on Supabase infrastructure located in Frankfurt, Germany (EU — eu-central-1), within the European Economic Area. No data is transferred outside the EEA without adequate safeguards.

06Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active. When you delete your account, all associated personal data — including your email address, spiritual practice history, Verse Wall posts, challenge records, and notes — is permanently deleted from our systems.

Certain anonymised aggregate statistics (e.g. total number of active users) may be retained for operational purposes; these do not identify you individually.

Backups may retain deleted data for up to 30 days following account deletion, after which they are purged automatically.

08Right to Deletion — How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your FaithFlow account and all associated data at any time, directly from within the app:

  1. Open FaithFlow and navigate to your Profile
  2. Tap Settings
  3. Tap Elimina account (Delete account)
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Deletion is immediate and permanent. This action triggers a server-side process that removes all personal data from our database, including your email address, password hash, prayer streak history, Bible reading records, Verse Wall posts, and challenge participation. This action cannot be undone.

If you are unable to access the app, you may also request account deletion by emailing faithflowsupport@gmail.com from the address registered to your account.

09Security

We implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data we process, including:

  • Passwords hashed using bcrypt via Supabase Auth — never stored or transmitted in plaintext
  • All data in transit encrypted via TLS 1.2 or higher
  • All data at rest encrypted via AES-256 on Supabase infrastructure
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) enforced at the database layer — each user can access only their own data
  • API keys stored as environment variables, not embedded in client code

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours and, where required, notify affected users without undue delay.
